# Summary across all four pages

The four-page extraction is complete. The most important takeaway for the redesign:

**The live site already has a strong didactic structure** — step-list-first, quick-check confirmations, pro-tips and watch-outs in context, sample integration, reference criteria. The Phase 3 feedback issues weren't that this structure was missing; they were that the **lesson page design didn't surface this structure visually** (no scannable step list at top, no sticky title, video-first instead of step-list-first).

The redesign's job is to take this strong existing didactic content and **rebuild the lesson page template** around it: step list at the top, video as secondary reference, Quick Check and Pro Tip as named components, the sample affordance promoted to a first-class slot, and the four-page sequence kept intact (intro / setup / make / clinical) rather than collapsed into a procedural walkthrough.

This means the v3/v4 prototype I built — with its single 4-step procedural walkthrough — is **structurally the wrong frame** for what Elemental actually teaches. It should be reworked to mirror the four-page model with each page following the new lesson-page template, before anything ships.
